# Maintenance

Start from a specific change or observed problem. Recover the existing method, its criteria and sources, the current artifacts, and the results whose conditions may still apply.

## Determine the affected part

Trace the change through what the method needs to answer:

- A changed requirement may alter the accepted outcome or supported scope.
- A data format or protocol change may alter inputs, parsing, legal variants, or the observation point.
- A changed dependency, device, runtime, or site may alter preparation, readiness, measurement, or cleanup.
- Documentation drift may leave an otherwise working operation hard to find or use.
- A tool defect may omit inputs, lose an observation, or assign the wrong result.
- A product regression may cause a sound method to report a real violation.

Use the affected path to choose the maintenance scope. Preserve valid requirements and reusable artifacts. Broaden the work when evidence connects the change to other judgments. A routine run that stays within the established method follows its existing instructions.

## Resolve the discrepancy

Compare the reported symptom with the criterion's authority and actual execution conditions. Identify whether the change is an accepted new behavior, a method defect, a product defect, or a missing condition.

For an accepted behavior change, update the affected criteria, inputs, instructions, or references with its basis. For a method defect, repair the operation or judgment and check the relevant valid, invalid, and legal-variation cases. For a product defect, preserve the failing input and observation, then route repair through the product's task and permission. Product instrumentation or other support changes need the same scope decision as other product work.

Resolve an unexplained failure before editing an assertion or reference output. Retain the original failed observation. Adopt a new baseline after establishing why it is acceptable.

## Recheck the change

Use the updated method on the affected path, following [Method validation](./method-validation.md) when validity needs closer examination. Bind new evidence to the updated object, method, and conditions. Reuse unchanged results with their original producer, versions, conditions, and scope stated.

In a constructed example, a feed contract changes dates from local calendar dates to UTC timestamps. Update the parser and time-boundary cases according to that decision. Preserve the unchanged requirement that record identifiers remain unique. If the feed repeats an identifier, save that violation for the feed owner. Changing the uniqueness check would need a separate requirement decision.

## Update the usable entry

Update the current instructions and links with the artifacts they describe. Remove replaced current entry points so users reach the supported method. Preserve historical evidence where its owner maintains it. Save the change basis, affected scope, method-check results, and remaining conditions with the method or owning task.

Deliver the updated location and operating conditions. Explain what existing users need to change and who owns any blocked continuation. Ongoing use supplies evidence for the next maintenance request.
