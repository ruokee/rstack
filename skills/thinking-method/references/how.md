# How

Build an evidence-grounded account of how something works at the level needed for the question.

## Method

1. Identify the behavior to explain, the relevant context, and the level of detail the reader needs.
2. Find the parts, roles, inputs, outputs, and state that determine that behavior. Consult the relevant records, implementation, observations, or domain sources.
3. Trace a concrete case through the interactions. Distinguish an observed case from an illustrative example.
4. Examine the conditions that change the outcome. Include feedback, dependencies, or failure paths when they explain the behavior in question.
5. Check the account against the available evidence. Identify the specific observation needed to resolve any consequential gap.

## Result

Explain the important interactions and their effect on the outcome. Give source locations when the reader needs to verify or continue the investigation. Use a diagram only when it makes the relationships easier to understand.

A list of components describes composition. A mechanism explanation also needs their interactions. An observed sequence alone does not establish causation.

## Completion

Finish when the reader can follow the relevant path and understand the conditions under which the explanation holds. Preserve any material uncertainty beside the affected claim.
