# Discuss

Develop a shared understanding that is sufficient for the next judgment or action.

## Method

1. Establish the question, the desired outcome, and the scope of the current discussion from what is already known.
2. Cover the important dimensions before drilling into one. Identify goals, situations, preferences, constraints, and disagreements that can change the direction.
3. Look up available facts. Ask the user about choices, experiences, or priorities that only they can supply. Group related questions and explain meaningful tradeoffs.
4. Use examples and brief restatements to check ambiguous terms or conflicting interpretations. Preserve corrections and explicit preferences in the working understanding.
5. Explore alternatives to the depth needed for the decision. Clearly distinguish proposals from decisions the user has made.

## Result

Present the useful conclusion or the next material question. When the discussion reaches a decision point, state what is agreed, what remains disputed or unknown, and who needs to decide or supply evidence.

Keep a discussion request within its authorized scope. Implementation, publication, and other consequential actions need their own permission. Save durable conclusions in an already authorized location when appropriate.

## Completion

Finish when the requested question is resolved sufficiently for its purpose, or when the next indispensable user choice is clear. Agreement can include an explicit account of a remaining disagreement.
