# Protect context

A useful working summary preserves the facts that could change the next decision. Keep bulky evidence recoverable at its source, and bring in the parts needed for the current question.

For example, a migration analysis of thousands of records may need the incompatible shapes, their frequencies, and examples tied to source locations. A summary such as "most records look normal" loses the exceptions that determine the migration. Preserve exact values and quotations when their form matters.

Use a focused search, query, or bounded extraction to obtain relevant material. Retain its source, selection conditions, conclusions, and open questions so a later contradiction can lead back to the details. Read complete material when the judgment requires its complete context.

Bulk extraction can be delegated under the task's role and tool rules when it has a bounded, checkable result. Keep consequential judgments with their assigned owner. Split processing by information needs while preserving the complete agreed scope.

Keep the goal and binding constraints available throughout the work. Load branch-specific detail when that branch becomes relevant. A shorter working context earns its value by retaining what is needed to finish correctly.
